Services

A menu built around care, not a clock.

Four disciplines, practised by a small team who work slowly and deliberately. Every appointment opens with a short consultation, so what you receive is considered rather than assumed.

01 — Nail Care

Manicure, pedicure & nail artistry

Precision shaping, careful cuticle work, and colour mixed to suit your skin tone rather than the season. Fine art and delicate press-on detailing are available for those who want something quietly more.

  • Signature manicure & pedicure
  • Gel and long-wear colour
  • Hand-painted nail artistry
  • Restorative nail treatments
02 — Hair & Colour

Cut, colour & considered styling

From precision cuts to hand-painted colour, every service begins with an honest conversation about what will actually suit your hair — not the boldest option in the book.

  • Cut & personalised styling
  • Hand-painted colour & balayage
  • Restorative colour correction
  • Blow-dry & finishing
03 — Spa Treatments

Slow rituals for hands, feet & scalp

Warm-oil scalp treatments, hand and foot massage, and paraffin therapy — additions meant to be felt as much as seen, taken at whatever pace your visit allows.

  • Warm-oil scalp ritual
  • Hand & foot massage
  • Paraffin therapy
  • Deep-conditioning hair masque
04 — Bridal & Occasion

For the days that ask a little more

Trial sessions, on-the-day styling, and full bridal-party care — planned well in advance so the morning of feels calm rather than rushed.

  • Bridal trial & consultation
  • On-the-day hair & nails
  • Bridal party scheduling
  • Touch-up kits for the evening
