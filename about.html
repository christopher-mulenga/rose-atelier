About Us

We started Rosé Atelier because good care shouldn't feel rushed.

Rosé Atelier began as a single room and a short list of clients who wanted the same thing we did: appointments that weren't stacked back to back, artists who remembered their hands and their hair, and a space that felt like it belonged to them for the hour they were in it. That room has grown, but the idea hasn't changed.

Today we're a small team of nail artists and hair stylists working under one roof, sharing one standard — that beauty, done properly, takes time, and time is what we give you.

Our Approach

Three ideas guide every appointment.

Listen First

Consultation before colour

No treatment begins until we understand what you actually want, and what will actually suit you — even when the two aren't quite the same thing.

Work Slowly

Unhurried, by design

We schedule fewer appointments per day than most salons, so no one — client or artist — is ever watching the clock.

Care Continues

Beyond the appointment

Aftercare guidance, gentle product recommendations, and a team who remembers your last visit when you book your next.

A Note From Us

You are looked after here, before you are styled.

That is the whole philosophy, really. Everything else — the colour, the cut, the finish — follows from it.
